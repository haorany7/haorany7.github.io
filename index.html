Haoran (Jensen) Yuan

PhD Student · Computer Engineering · University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign

My research focuses on efficient AI, especially on how to make embodied AI generalizable, scalable, and efficient. I build visuo-tactile world-action models that let robots reason about contact that cameras cannot see, and I develop parallel drafting methods that make large-model inference several times faster.

I am a PhD student in PLAN Lab at UIUC, advised by Prof. Ismini Lourentzou. I have also worked with BAIR at UC Berkeley, Hao AI Lab at UC San Diego, and MVIG at Shanghai Jiao Tong University. Before my PhD, I received an M.S. in Computer Engineering from UIUC, a dual B.S. from UIUC and Zhejiang University, and interned as a software engineer at Snowflake.

JetSpec++
JetSpec++: Foresight Parallel Drafting with Self Refinements
Zhaoxiang Feng*, Lanxiang Hu*, Haoran Yuan, Yu-Yang Qian, Jihui Yang, Peng Zhao, Yibo Zhu, Tajana Rosing, Hao Zhang
arXiv, 2026 Core Contributor
papercode

Conditions each drafted token on its full branch history and pre-drafts the next block during verification. Up to 7.45× faster than autoregressive decoding and 1.54× faster than DSpark on Qwen3-8B.

DexTacWAM: A Visuo-Tactile World-Action Model for Dexterous Manipulation
Haoran Yuan‡, Zekai Wang, Boning Shao, Haoran Lu, Trevor Darrell, Ismini Lourentzou, Wei Zhan
arXiv, 2026 Project Lead
project pagepapercodemodels & data

Extends a video diffusion world model with fingertip touch on a 22-DoF bimanual robot so it can predict contact the cameras cannot see. Reaches 70.6% average success on six real-robot tasks, against 38.0% for the strongest baseline.

JetSpec: Breaking the Scaling Ceiling of Speculative Decoding with Parallel Tree Drafting
Lanxiang Hu, Zhaoxiang Feng, Yulun Wu, Haoran Yuan, Yujie Zhao, Yu-Yang Qian, Bojun Wang, Peng Zhao, Daxin Jiang, Yibo Zhu, Tajana Rosing, Hao Zhang
arXiv, 2026 Core Contributor
project pagepapercodemodels

A causal draft head that emits a full speculative tree in one forward pass while keeping the target model's autoregressive factorization along every branch. Up to 9.64× end-to-end speedup on Qwen3-8B (MATH-500).

VTAM: Video-Tactile-Action Models for Complex Physical Interaction Beyond VLAs
Haoran Yuan*‡, Weigang Yi*, Zhenyu Zhang*, Wendi Chen*, Yuchen Mo, Jiashi Yin, Xinzhuo Li, Xiangyu Zeng, Chuan Wen, Cewu Lu, Katherine Driggs-Campbell, Ismini Lourentzou
arXiv, 2026 Project Lead
project pagepaper

Adds tactile streams to a pretrained video transformer through lightweight modality-transfer fine-tuning, with a tactile regularization loss that keeps vision from dominating. Reaches 90% average success on contact-rich tasks such as chip pick-and-place and cucumber peeling.
